What is Epic Haiku, and who is it for?
Assistant

Epic Haiku is a mobile application for healthcare providers that gives access to the Epic electronic health record from a smartphone or tablet.1 It covers patient information, messaging, and schedule management on the go.

It is one of the mobile tools documented on this site alongside Abridge Dictation, which is currently being piloted by specific departments.2

What is SlicerDicer called in axiUm?
Assistant

SlicerDicer is the Epic equivalent of axiUm's Info Manager.1

It is self-service, visualizes data, and can be customized — where Info Manager required a report request.
